Support

This page applies to the Apple Watch application that links here (the “App”). The App’s display name may change; this page is not tied to any specific product name.

What the App Offers
1. A charge-to-jump casual game built for Apple Watch
2. Hold to charge, release to leap onto the next platform
3. Perfect landings and combo bonuses for higher scores
4. Multiple visual themes
5. A local high-score list on your Watch
6. Optional aim line, on-screen hint, score popups, haptics, and landing shake
7. Pause during a run
8. English and Simplified Chinese, following the Watch language

Privacy
Gameplay, scores, theme choice, and settings stay on your Watch. We do not require an account, and we do not operate a server that stores your scores. Uninstalling the App removes local data.

Common Questions
1. How do I play?
Hold on the screen to charge, then release to jump. Land on the next platform to keep going. A round is short and can be paused at any time.

2. Why is there no sound?
watchOS has no game speaker for this App. Feedback uses Watch haptics, which you can turn off in Settings.

3. Where are my scores saved?
High scores and the local leaderboard are stored on this Watch only. They are not synced to iPhone or iCloud. Reset in Settings clears them. Deleting the App also clears them.

4. Can I turn off the dashed aim line or floating scores?
Yes. Open Settings in the App and toggle Aim Line, Hint, Score Popup, Haptics, or Jump Shake.

常见问题
1. 怎么玩？
按住屏幕蓄力，松手起跳。落到下一块台面即可继续。一局很短，随时可以暂停。

2. 为什么没有声音？
本应用在 watchOS 上不使用游戏扬声器。反馈通过手表触感完成，可在设置中关闭。

3. 分数存在哪里？
最高分和本地排行只存在这块手表上，不同步到 iPhone 或 iCloud。可在设置中重置。删除应用也会清除这些数据。

4. 能关掉引导虚线或飘分吗？
可以。打开应用内设置，开关引导线、提示、飘分、触感或落地抖动。
