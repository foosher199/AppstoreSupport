This app is built for field work, project documentation, and everyday check-in workflows. Its goal is simple: make watermarked photo capture smoother, clearer, and easier to use.

Instead of trying to be an overloaded “all-in-one camera suite,” the app focuses on what many users actually need:
live preview, rich watermark templates, flexible field editing, and one-tap save to the photo library.

Core Features
1. Capture photos with live watermark overlay
2. Import photos from the library and add watermarks
3. Multiple preset templates (engineering, attendance, travel, transport, and more)
4. Custom watermark templates and fields
5. Drag to reposition watermarks
6. Show time, location, coordinates, notes, and other fields (depending on template and permissions)
7. Save watermarked photos to the photo library
8. Membership unlocks capture & save, higher export resolution, and related benefits

Why This App
1. Focused product direction
Designed around “capture + watermark,” so you can start quickly without a steep learning curve.

2. Templates for many scenarios
Preset templates fit common workflows such as engineering, attendance, property, and transport.

3. Flexible editing
Adjust fields, order, and content, and save custom templates for reuse.

4. Local processing
Photos are composited on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

5. Transparent subscriptions
Plans, trials, and restore purchase are shown in-app; payments are handled securely by Apple.

Common Use Cases
1. On-site engineering documentation
2. Field attendance and check-in records
3. Property, home service, and transport workflows
4. Travel or event photo records

Permissions
1. Camera — capture watermarked photos
2. Photo Library — import and save results
3. Location (optional) — show place or coordinates in watermarks

Account and Subscription
1. No account registration required
2. Weekly, monthly, and yearly membership plans (as shown in the App)
3. Free trial may be available as shown in the App
4. Restore purchase and subscription management supported
