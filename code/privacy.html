Privacy Policy

Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Authenticator application and related services that link to this page from within the app, on App Store product pages, or on related websites (collectively, the “App”). The App provides two-factor authentication (TOTP) codes, a local password vault, optional iCloud backup, app lock with Face ID / Touch ID, and a custom keyboard extension for inserting verification codes.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. Related terms are in our Terms of Use (EULA).

Information We Collect

Authentication Secrets and Account Data

When you add two-factor accounts, the App stores issuer names, account labels, groups, and TOTP secrets (typically derived from QR codes or manual entry). Password entries you save (title, username, password, website, and group) are also stored for your use in the App.

This data is encrypted on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your OTP secrets or passwords.

Camera and Photo Library

With your permission, the App may use:

  • Camera — to scan QR codes that contain otpauth:// setup URLs
  • Photo Library (read) — to detect QR codes in images you select

Camera and photo access are used only to extract setup information on your device. We do not upload your photos or camera frames to our servers.

Biometrics and App Lock

If you enable app lock, Face ID or Touch ID is handled by Apple’s LocalAuthentication framework on your device. We do not receive your biometric data. Biometric matching stays on your device under Apple’s control.

iCloud Sync (Optional)

If you turn on iCloud backup/sync, an encrypted vault may be stored in your personal iCloud / CloudKit container associated with your Apple ID so you can restore data across your devices. That data is subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not have access to the contents of your iCloud account.

App Group and Keyboard Extension

The optional keyboard extension may read an encrypted shared vault via App Group / Keychain access groups so you can insert current TOTP codes into other apps. This sharing happens only on your device (and, if you enable it, via your iCloud sync). Full Access may be required for the keyboard to read the shared vault; you can revoke keyboard access in iOS Settings at any time.

Subscription and Purchase Information

If you subscribe to Premium, payment is processed by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We do not receive your payment card details. The App reads subscription status on your device to unlock Premium features (such as encrypted file export/import), including:

  • Product identifier and whether a subscription is active
  • Plan type (weekly or yearly, as shown in the App)
  • Transaction and entitlement information returned by Apple (for validation and restore purchases)

Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store the following data locally on your device:

  • Encrypted OTP accounts and password entries
  • App lock and preference settings
  • iCloud sync on/off state
  • Local Premium entitlement flags used to unlock features

Uninstalling the App removes local app data. Data already synced to your iCloud remains under your Apple ID until you delete it through Apple’s tools or turn off sync and clear related iCloud data.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Account registration data (the App does not require sign-in with us)
  • Your OTP secrets or passwords on our servers
  • Contacts, calendars, or other data unrelated to core features
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking data
  • Analytics through third-party analytics SDKs

How We Use Information

We use the information above only to:

  • Generate and display TOTP codes and manage your password vault
  • Scan QR codes to add accounts
  • Protect access with app lock
  • Sync or back up an encrypted vault when you enable iCloud
  • Power the keyboard extension when you enable it
  • Validate App Store subscriptions, restore purchases, and apply Premium benefits
  • Respond to support requests

Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Where Data Is Stored

Your authentication and password data stay on your device in encrypted form by default. Optional backups may live in your personal iCloud container or in encrypted files you export yourself. Except for Apple App Store subscription validation and optional Apple iCloud storage you enable, the App does not rely on our own cloud database.

How Long We Keep Data

  • Local vault data remains until you delete accounts or uninstall the App.
  • Exported backup files are under your control wherever you save or share them.
  • iCloud copies follow Apple’s retention for your Apple ID.

Deleting Data

You can delete individual accounts or password entries in the App, or delete the App from iPhone / iPad Storage in Settings to clear local data. Deleting the App does not cancel an App Store subscription; manage subscriptions in Apple ID settings.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is involved only in these cases:

  • Apple — App Store subscriptions, optional iCloud / CloudKit sync, camera and photo library permissions, and biometric unlock, subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • You — when you export a backup or use system share sheets to send a file you choose.
  • Legal requirements — when required by law or to protect users, our service, or the public.

Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and we do not show targeted advertising in the App.

Security

The App encrypts vault data on device (AES-GCM) and uses Apple Keychain / App Group mechanisms where applicable. You remain responsible for protecting your device passcode, biometrics, exported backups, and Apple ID. No app can guarantee perfect security.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will help address it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last Updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
