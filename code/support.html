Support

This app keeps two-factor codes and passwords on your device, ready when you need them.

Core Features

  1. Add accounts by scanning a QR code, picking a QR image, entering a secret, or importing a Google Authenticator export
  2. View time-based (TOTP) codes with a countdown and one-tap copy
  3. Organize accounts with groups and search
  4. Store passwords locally in an encrypted vault
  5. Generate strong passwords
  6. Lock the app with Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode
  7. Custom keyboard to insert current codes into other apps
  8. Premium unlocks encrypted iCloud sync and password-protected backup export/import

Privacy

OTP secrets and passwords are encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM). We do not run a server that stores your vault.

The keyboard's Full Access is used only to read the encrypted shared vault. It does not collect what you type.

Portable backups are encrypted with a password you choose. We cannot recover that password.

Subscriptions

  1. No account registration with us
  2. Premium is an auto-renewable subscription. The plan name, billing period, and price shown in the app before purchase are charged to your Apple ID. They may change and differ by storefront.
  3. No free trial unless a trial is shown on the purchase sheet
  4. Restore Purchases is available in the app; manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions
