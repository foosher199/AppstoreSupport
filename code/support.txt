This app is built for two-factor authentication and everyday account security. Its goal is simple: keep your TOTP codes and passwords easy to use, private on your device, and ready when you need them.

Instead of turning into an overloaded “security suite,” the app focuses on what many users actually need:
fast setup, clear codes, local encryption, optional iCloud backup, and a keyboard that can insert verification codes.

Core Features
1. Add accounts by scanning a QR code, picking a QR image, or entering a secret manually
2. View time-based (TOTP) codes with a countdown and one-tap copy
3. Organize accounts with groups and search
4. Store passwords locally in an encrypted vault
5. Generate strong passwords
6. Lock the app with Face ID / Touch ID or device passcode
7. Optional iCloud sync for encrypted vault backup across your devices
8. Custom keyboard extension to insert current codes into other apps
9. Premium unlocks encrypted file export/import and related backup benefits

Why This App
1. Focused product direction
Designed around authenticator codes + passwords, so you can start quickly without extra clutter.

2. Local-first privacy
OTP secrets and passwords are encrypted on your device. We do not run a server that stores your vault.

3. Practical everyday tools
Copy codes quickly, search accounts, and use the keyboard extension when typing into other apps.

4. Optional cloud backup
Turn on iCloud sync when you want recovery across your own Apple devices.

5. Transparent subscriptions
Plans and restore purchase are shown in-app; payments are handled securely by Apple.

Common Use Cases
1. Protecting email, social, and work accounts with 2FA
2. Keeping backup codes and passwords in one place on your phone
3. Restoring accounts on a new iPhone via iCloud or Premium export/import
4. Inserting a verification code from the keyboard without switching apps

Permissions
1. Camera — scan QR codes to add 2FA accounts
2. Photo Library — select QR code images
3. Face ID / Touch ID — unlock the app when App Lock is enabled
4. iCloud — optional encrypted sync (when enabled)
5. Keyboard Full Access — required for the keyboard extension to read the shared encrypted vault

Account and Subscription
1. No account registration required with us
2. Weekly and yearly Premium plans (as shown in the App)
3. Free trial may be available as shown in the App
4. Restore purchase and subscription management supported via Apple ID
