Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the iPhone and Apple Watch application and related pages that link here from within the app, on App Store product pages, or on related websites (collectively, the “App”). The App’s display name may change; this policy is not tied to any specific product name.

The App is a casual ritual-style game: you hold a question in mind, spin a compass, and reveal an answer card for entertainment and light inspiration. Preferences and related data are stored on your devices. We do not require an account, and we do not operate a server that stores your app content or preferences.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. Related terms are in our Terms of Use.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store the following data locally on your iPhone and/or Apple Watch:

  • Selected theme or category preference
  • Other simple preference flags used only for local app behavior
  • Temporary in-session state needed to run the ritual and show an answer card

When your iPhone and Apple Watch are paired, the App may use Apple’s Watch Connectivity features to send answer content between those devices so both can show a related result. That transfer is handled through Apple’s system frameworks between your own devices. We do not receive that content on developer-operated servers.

Uninstalling the App removes local app data from that device.

Photos (Optional, iPhone)

On iPhone, you may choose to save an answer card image to your Photo Library. If you do, the system may ask for permission to add photos. The App uses that permission only to save the card you chose to export. We do not browse your existing photos, and we do not upload card images to our servers.

Apple Watch does not offer card saving to Photos in the App.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Account registration or sign-in data (the App does not require an account with us)
  • Name, email, contacts, location, health, or payment information through the App
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking data
  • Analytics through third-party analytics SDKs
  • Voice recordings of your questions (questions stay in your mind; the App does not require you to type or speak them)

The App does not currently include in-app purchases, subscriptions, or ads. We do not sell your personal information.

Optional Links to Apple

If you choose to rate the App, your device may open Apple’s App Store review page. That visit is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive the content of your review from that flow.

If you email us for support, we receive whatever you choose to send (for example device model, iOS or watchOS version, and a description of the issue) only to respond to that request.

How We Use Information

Local data on your devices is used only to:

  • Remember your theme preference and related local settings
  • Run the compass ritual and show answer cards
  • Optionally save a card image to Photos when you ask the App to do so on iPhone
  • Optionally relay an answer between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch

Support email is used only to reply to the issue you reported.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is involved only in these cases:

  • Apple — App Store distribution, system photo-library saving you initiate, Watch Connectivity between your devices, and an optional review page you choose to open, subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • You — when you email us for support.
  • Legal requirements — when required by law or to protect users, our service, or the public.

Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and we do not show targeted advertising in the App.

Security

Local preferences use the device’s standard storage. Optional photo exports use Apple’s Photos frameworks on iPhone. You remain responsible for protecting your device passcode and Apple ID. No app can guarantee perfect security.

Children’s Privacy

The App is a casual entertainment experience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us and we will help address it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last Updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
