Support

This page applies to the iPhone and Apple Watch application that links here (the “App”). The App’s display name may change; this page is not tied to any specific product name.

What the App Offers
1. A casual ritual-style game for iPhone and Apple Watch
2. Hold a question in mind, spin the compass, and reveal an answer card
3. Multiple themes such as Life, Love, Growth, Study, Work, and Relationships
4. A short Notes screen explaining that the App is for entertainment only
5. On iPhone: optional saving of an answer card to Photos
6. On Apple Watch: the same core ask-and-reveal flow in a compact layout
7. English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese, following system language

How to Play
1. Choose a theme
2. Quietly think of a question
3. Tap Reveal (or the equivalent button in your language)
4. Watch the compass turn, then read the answer card
5. Ask again whenever you like

Privacy
Theme preference and related settings stay on your devices. We do not require an account, and we do not operate a server that stores your answers or preferences. On iPhone, saving a card uses Apple Photos with add-only permission when you choose to export. Uninstalling the App removes local app data from that device.

Important Reminder
This is a game. The compass, symbols, and answers are for fun and a little inspiration. They do not predict the future and are not medical, legal, financial, or relationship advice. What really matters is yours to decide.

Common Questions
1. Do I need to type my question?
No. Keep the question in your mind. The App does not require text or voice input for the question.

2. Can I save the answer card?
On iPhone, yes—use Save Card when the result is shown (Photos permission may be requested). Apple Watch does not save cards to Photos.

3. Why does the Watch app look different?
The Watch version is a compact companion experience with the same core ritual. Some iPhone-only actions, such as saving to Photos, are not available on Watch.

4. Does the App work offline?
Yes for the core experience. An internet connection is not required to spin the compass and reveal a card. App Store rating links and email support need network access on your side.

5. Will my theme choice sync everywhere?
Theme preference is stored locally on each device. Behavior may differ if you change settings separately on iPhone and Watch.

支持

本页适用于链接到此处的 iPhone 与 Apple Watch 应用（下称「本应用」）。应用显示名称日后可能调整，本页不绑定具体产品名。

功能
1. 面向 iPhone 与 Apple Watch 的仪式感休闲游戏
2. 心中默念问题，转动罗盘，揭晓答案卡片
3. 多种主题，如生活、爱情、成长、学业、职场、人际关系
4. 简短「说明」页，提示本应用仅供娱乐
5. iPhone：可选将答案卡片保存到相册
6. Apple Watch：同样的提问与揭晓流程（紧凑布局）
7. 英文、简体中文、繁体中文，跟随系统语言

怎么玩
1. 选择一个主题
2. 在心中默念你的问题
3. 点击「开启答案」（或你当前语言下的对应按钮）
4. 观看罗盘旋转，阅读答案卡片
5. 想再问一次时即可继续

隐私
主题偏好和相关设置保存在你的设备上。无需注册账号。我们没有用于保存答案或偏好的服务器。在 iPhone 上保存卡片时，若你选择导出，将使用系统相册的「添加照片」权限。卸载应用会清除该设备上的本机应用数据。

重要提醒
这是一款游戏。罗盘、符号和答案只为玩乐与启发，不是对未来的预测，也不是医疗、法律、财务或感情上的专业建议。当真的事，交给你自己。

常见问题
1. 需要把问题打出来吗？
不需要。把问题留在心里即可。本应用不要求输入文字或语音问题。

2. 可以保存答案卡片吗？
在 iPhone 上可以——结果页使用「存储卡片」（可能请求相册权限）。Apple Watch 不支持保存到相册。

3. 为什么手表版界面不一样？
手表版是紧凑的配套体验，核心仪式相同。部分仅 iPhone 的操作（如保存到相册）在手表上不可用。

4. 可以离线使用吗？
核心体验可以。转动罗盘与揭晓卡片不需要联网。打开 App Store 评分页或发送邮件支持时，需要你这一侧的网络。

5. 主题选择会到处同步吗？
主题偏好保存在各设备本机。若你在 iPhone 与手表上分别修改，表现可能不同。
