Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 18, 2026
Last Updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application that links to this page (the “App”). The App processes your photos, videos, contacts, and calendars on your device. We do not upload this content to developer-operated servers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how that information is used, and the choices available to you. The App does not require an account and does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. Related terms are available in our Terms of Use.

2. Information the App Accesses

2.1 Photos and Videos

If you grant Photos permission, the App accesses your photo library to display and analyze media, identify duplicate or similar photos, screenshots, Live Photos, screen recordings, and large videos, compress media you select, and delete items only after you confirm the action. This processing occurs on your device.

2.2 Contacts

If you grant Contacts permission, the App accesses your address book to identify duplicate or incomplete contacts, perform changes you confirm, and create VCF backup files you request. Backup files are stored in the App's local container and leave the device only if you choose to export or share them using Apple's system interface.

2.3 Calendars

If you grant Calendar permission, the App accesses calendar events to list past events and delete only the events you select and confirm. Calendar processing occurs on your device.

2.4 Device and Local App Information

The App may access or store the following information locally:

  • Available and total device storage, used to show storage information
  • Preferences, membership state, and feature history
  • Scan caches, thumbnails, and temporary processing files
  • Contact backup files and media files generated at your request

These items remain under the control of your device. Media saved to your Photos library remains there until you delete it, even if you uninstall the App.

2.5 Purchases and Subscriptions

Apple processes subscriptions and in-app purchases through StoreKit and the App Store. The App reads Apple-provided product and entitlement information to display offers, unlock paid features, and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment card details.

2.6 Support Communications

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address, message, and any information you choose to include. We use it only to respond to your request, maintain necessary support records, and address security or legal issues.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect or transmit to us:

  • Your photos, videos, contacts, calendars, or generated backups
  • Location, browsing history, advertising identifiers, or tracking data
  • Analytics, diagnostics, or usage profiles through third-party SDKs

4. How Information Is Used

Information accessed on your device is used only to provide the feature you initiate, such as analyzing media, compressing selected files, organizing contacts, creating a contact backup, displaying calendar events, or applying a deletion you confirm.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal content. Apple processes App Store purchases under Apple's Privacy Policy. When you intentionally use the system share sheet to export a backup or other file, the destination you choose receives that file under its own terms and privacy practices.

6. Retention and Deletion

We do not operate servers that store your photo library, contacts, calendars, scan results, or backup files. Local caches and files remain on your device until removed through the App, by iOS, or when the App is uninstalled. Items saved outside the App's container, including media in Photos or files you exported, must be deleted from their destination separately.

Support emails are retained only as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain business records, resolve disputes, or comply with law. You may request deletion by contacting us, subject to records we must retain for legal purposes.

7. Your Choices and Permissions

You can grant, limit, or revoke access in iOS Settings under the App's settings or under Privacy & Security for Photos, Contacts, and Calendars. Revoking permission may prevent the related feature from working. You control each deletion, edit, compression, backup, and export action initiated in the App.

8. Security

The App uses Apple's platform protections and processes protected content locally. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so you should protect access to your device and review files before sharing them.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child sent information to us in a support request, contact us so we can address it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App or legal requirements change. We will update the “Last Updated” date and provide additional notice when required by law.
