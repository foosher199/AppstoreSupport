1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use any of our applications that link to this page (each, an “App”). By using the App, you agree to this Policy. Related terms are in our Terms of Use.

Many of our Apps process content on your device. Some Apps may also offer optional cloud features, accounts, or subscriptions. What is shown in the App you are using controls which practices apply.

2. Information We May Collect or Access

2.1 Information We Do Not Seek by Default

Unless a feature below applies and you choose to use it, we do not collect:

  • Location data
  • Browsing history outside the App
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking data
  • Analytics through third-party advertising or analytics SDKs (unless an App discloses otherwise in its listing)

2.2 On-Device Content and System Permissions

Depending on the App, iOS may ask for permission to access data on your device so the App can provide features you request. Examples include (only if the App requests them):

  • Photos / Media Library — to find, preview, compress, organize, or delete media you choose
  • Contacts — to find duplicates, clean up, or export a backup you request
  • Calendars — to list or remove events you choose
  • Files / Documents — to import or export files you select
  • Camera or Microphone — only if the App offers capture features
  • Notifications — only if the App offers optional alerts

When processing is on-device, selected content is used locally to perform the task. We do not upload your photo library, contacts, or calendars to our servers unless the App clearly offers a cloud feature and you start that feature.

2.3 Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store locally, for example:

  • Preferences and settings (including membership status cached for offline use)
  • Caches or temporary files needed to complete a task
  • Keychain items (for example, session tokens), if the App supports sign-in

Uninstalling the App removes its local data on that device, subject to iOS behavior.

2.4 Account Information (If Sign-In Is Offered)

If the App offers Sign in with Apple or another account option, we may receive and store identifiers needed to run the account (such as an Apple user identifier, an internal account ID, and optionally your name or email if you share them). We do not receive your Apple ID password.

2.5 User Content Uploaded for Cloud Features (If Offered)

If you start a cloud task (for example, remote conversion or sync), the file or data you select may be uploaded to our servers or service providers solely to complete that task. We may store temporary originals/outputs and task metadata (such as file name, size, and status) under retention rules described below or in the App.

We do not use your uploaded content for advertising or to train machine-learning models.

2.6 Subscription and Purchase Information

If you subscribe or make an in-app purchase, payment is processed by Apple through StoreKit / the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. We or Apple may provide information needed to unlock benefits, such as product identifiers, transaction identifiers, and subscription status or expiration dates.

Products, prices, and free quotas may differ by App. What is shown in the App you are using controls.

3. How We Use Information

We use information only to:

  • Provide and improve App features you use
  • Process on-device or cloud tasks you start
  • Authenticate accounts and API requests, where applicable
  • Validate App Store purchases, restore purchases, and apply entitlements
  • Respond to support requests and address abuse or security issues
  • Comply with law when required

4. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

  • On-device data stays on your device unless you choose a cloud feature that uploads it.
  • Cloud task files (if any) are typically removed within a limited time after processing (often about 24 hours, or sooner if you delete the task or account), unless the App states a different retention period.
  • Account / subscription records (if any) are kept while needed to provide the service.

If the App provides account deletion, use Settings or Account → Delete Account where available. Deleting an account does not cancel an App Store subscription; manage subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only with:

  • Apple — for Sign in with Apple and App Store billing, under Apple’s Privacy Policy
  • Service providers — hosting or processing providers that help operate optional cloud features, bound to process data on our instructions
  • Legal requirements — when required by law or to protect users, our service, or the public

6. Tracking and Advertising

We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies for advertising, and we do not show third-party targeted ads in the App unless an App listing states otherwise.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the App (for example, HTTPS for network traffic, Keychain for sensitive tokens). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it where applicable.

9. International Users

If cloud or account features are used, information may be processed on servers outside your country. By using those features, you understand that transfers may occur.
