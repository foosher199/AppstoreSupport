This support page is shared by our iOS apps that link here. App display names and feature sets may differ. Use the in-app Settings or Help entry for the product you installed; the Privacy Policy and Terms linked below apply to all apps that point to this folder.

What Our Apps May Offer
Depending on the app, features can include:
1. On-device photo or video cleanup, compression, or organization
2. Contacts cleanup or backup
3. Calendar cleanup of past events
4. Document or file utilities (where offered)
5. Optional cloud processing (where offered)
6. In-app subscriptions or purchases via the App Store

Privacy-First Design
1. Many tools run on your device; content is not uploaded unless you start a cloud feature.
2. System permissions (Photos, Contacts, Calendar, etc.) are requested only when needed.
3. We do not sell your personal information.

Subscriptions
1. Plan details and prices are shown in the app before purchase.
2. Restore purchases from the paywall or Settings.
3. Manage or cancel auto-renewable subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings.
4. Refunds are handled by Apple under App Store policies.

Limited Photo Access
If you grant “Selected Photos” access, only those items are visible to the app. You can change the selection in iOS Settings → [App Name] → Photos, or use in-app “Select More Photos” when available.

Common Questions
1. Why do I still see photos I selected earlier?
iOS remembers Limited Library selections until you change them in Settings or the system picker.

2. Did deleting in the app remove my files permanently?
Deleted photos/videos go to the system Recently Deleted album for a period; contacts and calendar deletions follow iOS behavior. Review confirmations carefully.

3. Compression created a new file?
Many apps save a compressed copy and keep the original unless you choose to delete it.
